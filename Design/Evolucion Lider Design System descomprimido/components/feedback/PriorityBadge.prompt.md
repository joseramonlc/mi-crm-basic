Priority indicator — full pill or a bare colored dot (used on prospect avatars).

```jsx
<PriorityBadge level="high" />
<PriorityBadge level="medium" />
<PriorityBadge level="low" dotOnly />
```

Levels: `high` (Alta, red) · `medium` (Media, amber) · `low` (Baja, green). Use `dotOnly` for compact contexts.
